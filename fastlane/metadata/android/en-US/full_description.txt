Stand changes a phone on a stand or a charger into a clock display.

<b>Themes</b>
12 themes: Tactical, Orbit, Flip, Minimal, Space, Dots, LED, Words, Analog, Neon, Mega and Radar. Swipe left or right to change the theme. Swipe up or down to change the colour.

<b>Your style</b>
* 11 colours and a slider for your own colour
* Glow, colour gradient and 7 clock fonts
* Animated backgrounds: stars, fireflies, neon grid, waves, pulse
* Date, battery level and a line of your own text
* A live preview in the settings

<b>Made for a stand</b>
* The app reads the tilt sensor and turns the picture itself. It works when auto-rotate is off.
* It can be the system screensaver, which starts when the device charges.

<b>Low power</b>
* A black background with few lit pixels, for AMOLED screens
* On battery, the screen updates one time each second or each minute. The animation is smooth only when the device charges, unless you change this.
* A timer lets the screen go off on battery
* Pixel shift prevents screen burn-in
* Night mode: dim or red from 22:00 to 06:00

<b>Privacy</b>
The app has no internet permission, no advertisements and no trackers.
